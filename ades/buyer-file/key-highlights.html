Site Challenges

✈️

Airport-Adjacent LocationIdeal positioning, but strict Aviation Authority regulations limited the height of any new structure on the terrace.

📏

Height RestrictionsAirport Authority clearance requirements capped vertical additions, ruling out conventional rooftop builds.

🏗️

27,000 Sq.ft of Unused SpaceThe entire terrace lay idle — generating zero revenue and offering no guest value.

⚡

Tangled Utility ServicesADES had to clear, reroute, and rationalise all existing MEP utility services before any transformation could begin.

🧱

25-Year-Old ChimneysAgeing chimney structures occupied prime terrace area. ADES engineered their safe removal and structural remediation.

What ADES Delivered

🌿

Revenue-Generating Guest SpaceThe previously dead terrace is now a premium hospitality destination — directly added to the property's income sheet.

🎪

Outdoor Lawn for EventsA landscaped open-air lawn now hosts private events, banquets, and corporate gatherings year-round.

📈

Significantly Increased Client ValueThe transformation delivered a measurable uplift in asset valuation, occupancy appeal, and guest satisfaction scores.
